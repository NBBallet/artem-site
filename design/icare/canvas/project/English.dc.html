The ballet
L'Envol d'Icare

In this myth, today, I see a possibility

The myth warns: do not fly too high, or the sun will melt your wings. Our Icarus holds the whole universe in his heart — the flight, the sun, the father and the fall are one.

This line of thought interests me all the more because Matisse has already given it form in his modern Icarus: the sun is inside, and the figure seems to levitate, like an astronaut in a space shuttle.

“The work still awaits its dance premiere.”
Boosey & Hawkes, dance catalogue

Patrons, foundations, grant programmes

Partnering in a world choreographic premiere

The score Markevitch wrote for the Paris Opera has been waiting almost a hundred years for its choreography. We are looking for patrons, foundations and grant programmes to bring it back to the French stage with us — from preparing the production to the premiere and the centenary seasons, 2029–32.
